# Code of conduct

PMP Prep Suite is a study aid made by one person and used by people preparing
for a hard exam. Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews) is expected to help keep it a friendly,
useful place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to project management, to
  GitHub, or to English.
- Criticise questions, code and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Give reasons and sources when you say an answer is wrong, so it can be
  checked.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into the app.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting real PMI exam questions, or other material you have no right to
  share.
- Spam and advertising.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, send a private message to the
maintainer, [Jahangir Babayev on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jahangir-babayev-a432a5145). Reports are kept private.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and
block people from the project, temporarily or for good, depending on what
happened.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
